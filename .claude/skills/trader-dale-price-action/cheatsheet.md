# Cheatsheet — Trader Dale's Decision Rules

## Core Filter
Every entry needs: (1) a Price Action signature (sideways accumulation / aggressive initiation / strong rejection, Ch3) AND (2) one of the 3 Volume Setups (Accumulation / Trend / Rejection, Ch13). Never enter on Volume alone or Price Action alone. Extra confirmations (support-flip, open-drive, AB=CD, session/daily open) raise conviction but aren't required.

## Entry Decision Rules
| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Level had 2+ strong reactions, now breached | Trade the retest in the flipped direction |
| Sideways range → strong one-sided move | Mark the move's origin as new S/R; wait for retest |
| Swing extreme is fast + sharp | Strong — trust it, anchor trades near it |
| Swing extreme is slow + indecisive | Weak — expect price to shoot through; never trade against it |
| Failed auction beyond your PT | Extend PT just past it (price is drawn through) |
| Failed auction against your position | Skip the trade |
| Price shoots through level, zero reaction | Wait for retest, take a reversal trade (opposite direction) |
| Price reacts even 1-2 pips before failing | NOT a reversal — level worked, just early |

## Order Type & Sessions
Market order: confirmed non-news spike into a level, or a weak-high/low rotation (better entry). Limit order: normal approach, and always during Asian session (no tested-level filtering, unattended). Skip a spike entry if it's news-driven.

## Stop-loss / Profit Target
- **Intraday default**: 10 pip PT / 12 pip SL (author's own; 20/20 also works for others — consistency matters more than the exact number).
- **ATR sizing**: ATR × 10,000 = avg daily volatility in pips; intraday SL/PT ≈ 10-20% of that. Skip a trade if the nearest volume-based PT zone is <10% of daily ATR away.
- **Swing**: SL behind volume-zone edge / low-volume area, roughly 0.5-4x daily ATR. **Long-term**: hundreds-thousands of pips behind a strong volume barrier, target ~1:1 RRR.
- **Trailing (Neutral style, default)**: move SL to the reaction point at +7 pips (counter-trend or a sharp confirming reaction) or +7.5-8 pips (with-trend / no trend). Avoid Conservative (breakeven-at-70-80%) — cuts winners short. Aggressive (never move SL) only when unable to monitor (e.g., Asian session).

## Tested-Level Check
Discard if: ≥8 pip reaction started within 0-3 pips of the level, OR the zone is wide and was tested anywhere inside it, OR the reaction was sharp/high-energy, OR it's counter-trend. Keep if: zone is narrow and the near-miss was outside it, OR the prior turn was slow, OR it's with-trend. Otherwise: use discretion.

## Macro News Tiers
| Tier | Examples | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Weak red | Oil Inventories, Unemployment Claims, Retail Sales | Safe to hold; re-enter 1-5 min after |
| Standard red | CPI, GDP, NFP, Unemployment Rate | Never hold intraday; cancel counter-trend positions |
| Monster red | Rate Decision, FOMC | Avoid for hours before; resume next session; never catch the high/low directly |

Indirect links: Oil→CAD, world-danger news→JPY (safe haven), dairy auctions→NZD, metals→CAD/AUD, China news→AUD. Exit 2-5 min before any significant release, at the nearest 1-min chart channel edge.

## Money Management
Risk/trade = (max tolerable drawdown %) ÷ (worst backtested losing streak × 1.2). E.g. 25% ÷ (6×1.2≈7) = **3.58%**. Never cut risk % during a drawdown — only raise it at a new equity high. Fix size per trade category (e.g. 2% intraday/1% reversal/3% swing), never by feel. Cut size on correlated simultaneous setups.

**Drawdown response**: normal (within history) → trade as usual. Excessive (30-50%+ beyond worst-ever) → halve size, don't stop, return to full size after recovering ~halfway.

## Profile Shape Quick-Read
D = balanced/rotation (short near high, long near low, POC = PT). P = aggressive buyers/weak sellers (POC/thin-cluster = support). b = aggressive sellers/weak buyers (POC/thin-cluster = resistance). Thin = fast trend (clusters = support in uptrend, resistance in downtrend).

## Pair Ranking (author's preference)
EUR/USD (best: cheap, liquid, precise) > USD/JPY (precise but spiky) > AUD/USD (slow, precise, China-dependent) > USD/CAD (oil-reactive, sometimes imprecise) > GBP/USD (avoid — spikes past S/R, poor fit for level trading).

## Session & Discipline Rules
Take every valid setup regardless of the day's P/L so far. Always exit before weekly close. At daily close (5pm ET), exit only if near PT/SL. Treat a gap landing on your level as confirmation, not a warning. Never delete old levels (market can react 30-40+ days later).

## Four Kinds of Trades
Judge process, not outcome: **Good win** (proper analysis + execution + wins) is the goal. **Bad win** (sloppy but wins) is the most dangerous — reinforces bad habits. **Good loss** (proper process, still loses — a ~60-70% strategy loses often) is normal; don't abandon the system. **Bad loss** (sloppy and loses) is cheap tuition — learn and move on.

## Red Flags
A "perfect-looking" trade → be more cautious, not less. Equity curve of small gains + one catastrophic dip → Martingale, abandon immediately. Feeling invincible after a streak → increase vigilance. Wanting to cut size mid-drawdown → don't, it extends recovery. Broker with 2-3 pip spreads on majors → switch, not viable for this method.
