# Chapter 17: Market Analysis from A to Z

## Core Idea
This chapter is the author's end-to-end daily workflow that stitches together every framework so far — a fixed sequence of checks (news → overall trend → Price Action → Volume Profile setups → confirmations → weak-point/failed-auction screen) that intraday analysis follows in full and swing/long-term analysis follows in a shortened form.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Intraday analysis sequence** (author's exact order):
  1. Check the macro calendar and mark upcoming news times (Ch16) — avoid being caught in a news-triggered spike.
  2. Assess the overall trend on a 240-minute or Daily chart: strong uptrend → only take longs; strong downtrend → only take shorts; rotation → both directions are fair game. Rationale: intraday levels aren't strong enough to stop or reverse a multi-day trend.
  3. Do a Price Action pass (author uses 30-minute, or 15-minute/1-hour as alternatives): mark sideways areas, aggressive initiation, and strong rejections (Ch3).
  4. Run Volume Profile analysis on the significant Price Action areas found in step 3, checking for the three core setups (Ch13): Accumulation, Trend, Rejection.
  5. Cross-check each volume-based level against the "Confirmation strategies": Support-becomes-resistance, Open-drive, AB=CD, Session Open, Daily/weekly high-low (Ch4-Ch9).
  6. Screen every finished level for a nearby weak high/low (Ch10) or failed auction (Ch11) and avoid placing entries near either.
  7. Execute the trading plan without deviation.
- **Swing/long-term analysis sequence** (abbreviated version): skip steps 1 (macro calendar) and 2 (overall trend check) — swing/long-term SL distances are wide enough that ordinary news volatility won't threaten the trade, and swing/long-term levels are considered strong enough to justify counter-trend entries. Everything else (Price Action pass, Volume Profile setups, a narrower confirmation set of Support-becomes-resistance/Open-drive/AB=CD, and the weak-point/failed-auction screen) is done identically, just on higher timeframes — 240-minute/Daily for swing, Weekly/Monthly for long-term.

## Key Concepts
- **Exception for swing/long-term news risk**: central-bank-related news is called out as the one macro category that can still endanger a swing position despite the wider SL buffer.
- **Counter-trend swing entries**: because swing/long-term levels are judged strong enough to reverse even a strong trend, the author is comfortable entering counter-trend on those timeframes (unlike intraday, where trend alignment is enforced).

## Mental Models
- Think of the workflow as top-down filtering: macro risk → trend context → structure (Price Action) → precision (Volume Profile) → confirmation → hazard screen — each step narrows down to the final tradeable level.
- Treat the weak-high/low and failed-auction screen as a final "before you commit" gate, applied after a level is otherwise fully built.

## Anti-patterns
- **Taking counter-trend intraday trades**: explicitly avoided — intraday levels are considered too weak against a multi-day trend, unlike swing/long-term levels.
- **Skipping the confirmation-strategy cross-check**: a volume-based level without Price Action confirmation is treated as lower conviction.
- **Placing an entry level right next to a weak high/low or a failed auction**: screened out as a final step regardless of how good the level otherwise looks.

## Worked Example
Swing trade, AUD/USD Daily chart: the broader market was in a clear, strong downtrend. Because this was being planned as a swing trade rather than an intraday trade, the author entered a counter-trend long position anyway — a decision this same workflow would forbid on an intraday timeframe (step 2 above), but permits on swing/long-term timeframes where the level itself is judged strong enough to outweigh the prevailing trend.

## Key Takeaways
1. Follow the fixed sequence for intraday: macro calendar → overall trend → Price Action → Volume Profile setups → confirmation strategies → weak-point/failed-auction screen → execute.
2. Respect the overall trend for intraday trades (trade only with it); swing/long-term levels are treated as strong enough to justify counter-trend entries.
3. Skip the macro-calendar and trend-context steps for swing/long-term analysis, except stay alert to central-bank news specifically.
4. Always finish with the weak-high/low and failed-auction screen before finalizing any level, regardless of timeframe.
