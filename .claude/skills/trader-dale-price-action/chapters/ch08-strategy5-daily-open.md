# Chapter 8: Strategy 5 — Daily Open

## Core Idea
The Daily Open — the New York close/new-day boundary at 5:00 p.m. NY time — is, in the author's judgment, the single strongest "session open" type support/resistance level, traded the same way as Session Open but with higher conviction.

## Frameworks Introduced
- **Daily Open S/R**: the price level at the daily open (5:00 p.m. NY time) becomes support or resistance depending on the direction price moves after that boundary.
  - When to use: as a confirmation layer for the author's main volume-based strategies (not traded standalone).
  - How:
    1. Mark the Daily Open at 5:00 p.m. NY time.
    2. Wait to see a few candles form above or below the open.
    3. If price goes up after the daily open, the open level becomes support.
    4. If price goes down after the daily open, the open level becomes resistance.
    5. Watch for reactions when price retraces to this level intraday.

## Key Concepts
- **Daily Open time**: 5:00 p.m. New York time — the point the author defines as where "one day ends and a new day starts" for forex.
- **Strongest of the "open" levels**: the author explicitly ranks Daily Open above Session Open in strength as a support/resistance reference.

## Mental Models
- Treat Daily Open the same way as Session Open, but weight it more heavily — it's the author's preferred level of this type.

## Anti-patterns
- **Trading Daily Open as a standalone strategy**: like Session Open, the author uses this only as confirmation, not as a primary trade trigger.

## Key Takeaways
1. Mark the 5:00 p.m. NY daily open on every chart as a candidate S/R reference.
2. Direction after the open (up = support, down = resistance) determines how to read the level.
3. Use as a confirmation layer stacked with volume-based signals, not as a standalone entry trigger.
