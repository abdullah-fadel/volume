## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

**The central thesis**: ~80-90%+ of daily forex volume is controlled by a handful of large institutions. Retail traders can't move or predict markets directly — the entire system here is about reading institutional footprints (Price Action) and confirming them precisely (Volume Profile), then trading in alignment with "smart money" rather than guessing against it.

**Aggression drives price** — price moves because one side uses MARKET ORDERS to get filled immediately, not because of "more buyers than sellers." Read price as continuous flow/sentences, not isolated candlestick patterns.

**Three signs of institutional activity** (the core Price Action read, applied to every chart first):
1. **Sideways price action area** — institutions quietly accumulating a large position.
2. **Aggressive initiation activity** — the trend that follows accumulation, revealing direction.
3. **Strong rejection** — a sudden, aggressive reversal that marks new support/resistance.

**Price Action confirmation strategies** (used to confirm volume-based levels, some tradeable standalone):
- **Support becoming resistance (and vice versa)**: a breached strong level flips role; trade the retest, not the breakout.
- **Open-drive**: a sharp one-sided move's origin point becomes S/R; wait for the pullback retest.
- **AB=CD**: wave pattern (Fibonacci-measured CD leg = AB leg) projecting entry point D; 30-min+ only.
- **Session Open / Daily Open**: the open of a session (or the 5pm ET daily boundary) becomes S/R depending on which way price first moves.
- **Daily/weekly high-low breach-and-flip**: don't fade untested highs/lows directly (false-breakout prone) — wait for a confirmed breach, then trade the retest of the flipped level.
- **Strong vs. weak highs/lows**: sort every swing extreme by aggression (fast/decisive = strong, trust it; slow/indecisive = weak, expect price to shoot through it). Never place a vulnerable trade level next to a weak point.
- **Failed auction**: a swing extreme with multiple unresolved participants acts like a magnet, drawing price back through it — extend Profit Targets past nearby failed auctions; avoid entries with one against your position.

**The 3 core Volume Profile setups (never enter without one)**:
1. **Accumulation Setup**: find the heaviest-volume price inside a sideways range that preceded a strong move; enter on first touch.
2. **Trend Setup**: find the heaviest-volume price inside a volume cluster within a strong trend; enter with the trend on first touch. (Confluence with support-becomes-resistance = author's most powerful setup.)
3. **Rejection Setup**: find the heaviest-volume price within a strong rejection; enter on retest. (Author's hardest setup to master.)
- **Reversal trade**: if a level fails outright (no reaction at all), flip direction on the retest.
- **Profile shapes**: D (balanced/accumulating), P (aggressive buyers/weak sellers), b (aggressive sellers/weak buyers), Thin (fast strong trend) — each has different key levels (see cheatsheet).

**Position management**: Fixed PT/SL (ATR-based, e.g. 10/12 pips intraday) for simplicity, or Volume-based PT/SL anchored to the nearest significant volume zone for precision. Trail SL to the reaction point at ~70-80% of PT (Neutral style, author's default) rather than to breakeven (Conservative — explicitly discouraged, cuts winners short).

**Money management**: Risk per trade = (max tolerable drawdown %) ÷ (backtested worst losing streak × 1.2). Never lower risk % during a drawdown; rebase only at new equity highs. Cut size on correlated simultaneous setups.

**Trading psychology**: judge every trade by process (analysis + execution vs. plan), not outcome — a "good losing trade" is normal; a "bad winning trade" is the most dangerous because it reinforces broken process. Avoid the Cycle of Doom and Despair (strategy-hopping before mastering any one approach).

**Validation**: use the 5-Phase method (rough backtest → thorough backtest → micro live trading → half positions → full positions) rather than jumping straight to full size, and expect an early losing streak ("trial by fire") at full size.
