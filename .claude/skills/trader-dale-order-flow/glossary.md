# Glossary — Trader Dale Order Flow

**Absorption** — Heavy Bid+Ask volume at an S/R zone while price fails to break through; one side soaking up the other's pressure (Ch 9).

**Active (Aggressive) Market Participant** — Enters with a market order, filled immediately, accepting possible slippage (Ch 2).

**ATR (Average True Range)** — Volatility indicator used to size Stop Loss to ~10–20% of average daily range (Ch 11).

**Ask** — Right side of a footprint; contracts traded via Market Buy or Limit Sell (Ch 2, Ch 3).

**Auction Process** — Every new high/low results from an "auction" that should end with zero contracts on the opposite side; unresolved = Unfinished Business (Ch 4).

**b-Shaped Profile** — Volume Profile shape signaling a downtrend, or a rotation ending an uptrend (Ch 12).

**Bid** — Left side of a footprint; contracts traded via Market Sell or Limit Buy (Ch 2, Ch 3).

**Cumulative Delta** — Running daily sum of Delta, resetting each session; rises with net aggressive Buying, falls with net aggressive Selling (Ch 3, Ch 4).

**D-Shaped Profile** — Most common Volume Profile shape; temporary balance while institutions build positions (Ch 12).

**Delta** — Ask minus Bid for one footprint; positive = stronger Buyers, negative = stronger Sellers (Ch 3).

**DOM (Depth of Market)** — Shows pending orders; author avoids it since most pending orders never fill (Ch 1).

**Double / Triple Node** — A Multiple HVN made of two or three stacked HVNs (Ch 4).

**Failed Auction** — Synonym for Unfinished Business (Ch 4, Ch 8).

**First Touch / First Test Rule** — Trade an S/R level only on its first retest; a second reaction is less likely (Ch 6, Ch 7).

**Fixed Stop Loss** — Same SL distance on every trade regardless of conditions (Ch 11).

**Flexible Volume Profile** — Volume Profile applied to a hand-selected range (e.g., one rejection) instead of a full session (Ch 13).

**Footprint** — Candle replacement showing actual Bid/Ask volume traded at each price level within it (Ch 3).

**Footprint Summary Panel** — Panel below the chart; author limits his to Delta, Cumulative Delta, Volume (Ch 3).

**Green Cell** — Footprint cell where Ask exceeds Bid at that price (Ch 3).

**High Volume Node (HVN)** — Price within a footprint with the heaviest volume, marked with a black outline (Ch 3, Ch 4).

**Iceberg Order** — Large institutional order split into many small pieces so it evades the Trades Filter (Ch 4).

**Imbalance** — Cell where one side's volume is ≥300% of the diagonally opposite side; Buying (blue, Ask) or Selling (blue, Bid) (Ch 4).

**Level 1 Data** — Bid/Ask price and size data; minimum feed quality Order Flow needs (Ch 1).

**Level 2 Data** — Full market depth (order book); not needed for Order Flow (Ch 1).

**Low Volume Area** — SL zone just behind a heavy-volume area; price reaching it signals momentum against the trade (Ch 11).

**Multiple Node** — 2+ consecutive footprint HVNs at the same price, auto-highlighted yellow; an S/R zone (Ch 3, Ch 4, Ch 6).

**P-Shaped Profile** — Volume Profile shape signaling an uptrend, or a rotation ending a downtrend (Ch 12).

**Passive Market Participant** — Enters with a limit order, waits for price to arrive (Ch 2).

**Red Cell** — Footprint cell where Bid exceeds Ask at that price (Ch 3).

**Risk/Reward Ratio (RRR)** — Potential gain vs. loss; SL=10/TP=10 → RRR=1; SL=10/TP=20 → RRR=2 (Ch 11).

**Snowball Effect** — A passive confirmation (Limit Order/Absorption) quickly followed by an active one (Aggressive Orders/Delta) as more traders pile on (Ch 10).

**Stacked Imbalance** — 3+ same-direction Imbalances stacked; highlighted green (Support) or red (Resistance) (Ch 4, Ch 7).

**Strong Rejection** — Price moves aggressively one way, then suddenly reverses aggressively the other way (Ch 6, Ch 13).

**Support/Resistance (S/R)** — A price zone (not exact tick) where price is expected to react (Ch 4, Ch 9).

**Thin Profile** — Volume Profile shape signaling a strong trend with only small "bump" clusters (Ch 12).

**Total Volume** — Bid+Ask combined; the only split most Forex feeds reliably give (Ch 1, Ch 4).

**Trades Filter** — Hides fills below a lot-size threshold, isolating the largest single orders; misses Iceberg orders (Ch 4, Ch 7).

**Trailing Take Profit** — Extending a winning exit while Imbalances favor your side; stopped once a confirmation fires against you (Ch 11).

**Unfinished Business** — A high/low formed without the opposite side reaching zero contracts; behaves like a magnet (Ch 4, Ch 8).

**Volume Accumulation** — A rotation area where heavy volume was traded as institutions built a position pre-trend (Ch 13).

**Volume at Price** — What Volume Profile shows (volume by price level, not by time) (Ch 12).

**Volume Cluster** — A heavy-volume area within a trend or rejection, shown via darker shading (Ch 4, Ch 6, Ch 13).

**Volume Profile** — Indicator showing Volume at Price (Price+Time+Volume); locates institutional S/R zones (Ch 12).

**Volume Profile Shapes** — D (balance), P (uptrend/downtrend-ending), b (downtrend/uptrend-ending), Thin (strong trend) (Ch 12).

**6E** — CME EUR/USD futures contract; author's primary instrument and Futures proxy for EUR/USD (Ch 1).
