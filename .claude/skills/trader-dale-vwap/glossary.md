# Glossary

**1st VWAP deviations ("bands")** — two lines plotted above and below a date-anchored VWAP, calculated as standard deviations of it; horizontal slope signals rotation, vertical slope signals trend (Ch10, Ch11).

**Absorption** — an Order Flow confirmation where large volume appears simultaneously on both Bid and Ask sides of the footprint at a level, meaning one side's aggression is fully absorbed by the other; signals a likely reversal (Ch13).

**Anchor point** — the chosen starting candle/time for a VWAP calculation; determines which "average participant" the resulting line represents (Ch2).

**Anchored VWAP** — VWAP calculated from a chosen starting point other than a fixed calendar boundary — e.g., a swing point, trend start, news candle, heavy volume zone, gap, or earnings release (Ch1–Ch9).

**ATR (Average True Range)** — volatility indicator used to size Take Profit and Stop Loss as a percentage of average daily volatility; set to a 200-period on Daily data, then multiplied by 10,000 for pips (Ch14, Ch15).

**Barrier** — a strong Support/Resistance zone (from Price Action, VWAP, or Volume Profile) that price should not cross without signaling the trade thesis is wrong; the shared concept underlying Take Profit and Stop Loss placement (Ch14, Ch15).

**Combined barrier** — two or more overlapping barrier types (e.g., heavy volume zone + swing point) used together for the strongest possible Stop Loss placement (Ch15).

**Confluence** — when two or more independent trading setups (VWAP, Price Action, Volume Profile) converge on the same general Support/Resistance zone, raising conviction (Ch12).

**Cumulative Delta** — a running total of aggressive buying minus aggressive selling, derived from Order Flow; divergence from price near a VWAP-based level signals a likely turn (Ch13).

**Daily VWAP** — VWAP anchored at the start of the trading day (Asian session open), ending at the US session close; the author's preferred intraday timeframe is 5-minute (Ch3).

**Deviation development lag** — the tendency for 1st VWAP deviations to start as a tight, undeveloped channel at the start of a new anchor period, requiring patience before trading them (Ch10, Ch11).

**Drawdown** — the largest peak-to-trough loss (measured as consecutive losing trades) a strategy produced during backtesting; the basis of the risk-per-trade formula (Ch17).

**Earnings-anchored VWAP** — VWAP anchored at a stock's earnings-release candle; a specialized, stocks-only case of gap anchoring, since earnings often produce the largest gaps (Ch9).

**Fair price** — the author's shorthand for the VWAP value; the price level the "average" market participant (including institutions targeting "VWAP or better" fills) is already positioned around (Ch1, Ch2).

**Footprint chart** — an Order Flow visualization showing executed Bid (aggressive sellers, left) and Ask (aggressive buyers, right) volume at each price (Ch13).

**Gap** — a difference between one candle's close and the next candle's open; structurally more common in stocks than in continuously-traded forex; a valid VWAP anchor point when large (Ch8).

**Heavy volume zone** — a price area where Volume Profile shows unusually concentrated traded volume, typically formed during institutional accumulation; used both as a VWAP anchor point and as a Stop Loss/Take Profit barrier (Ch7, Ch14, Ch15).

**Limit Order confirmation** — an Order Flow signal where a single unusually large order appears on one side of the footprint at a level, inferred as an institutional Limit order defending that price (Ch13).

**Order Flow confirmation** — the author's preferred, most advanced trade-entry confirmation method, using footprint/Cumulative Delta analysis to verify institutional activity at a level before entering (Ch13).

**Position sizing (fixed)** — using the same account-risk percentage on every trade regardless of subjective confidence, with only the SL/TP pip distance varying trade to trade (Ch17).

**Reaction Point** — the swing point formed by a confirming candle when entering "after a successful reaction"; the natural location for Stop Loss, and the SL basis for the Start Simple template (Ch13, Ch16).

**Risk per trade formula** — (max tolerable drawdown %) ÷ (backtested worst losing streak × 1.2); the author's method for deriving a fixed percentage of account equity to risk per trade (Ch17).

**Rotation zone** — a sideways, low-directional-movement period, often with heavy volume, where institutions quietly accumulate a position ahead of a subsequent trend (Ch7, Ch10).

**RRR (Risk-Reward Ratio)** — ratio of potential loss to potential gain on a trade; the Start Simple template targets a fixed RRR of 1 (Ch16).

**Session boundaries** — the Daily VWAP's calculation window, from Asian-session open to US-session close; discarded and restarted each new day (Ch3).

**Start Simple template** — the author's recommended beginner combination: enter after a confirmed reaction, place Stop Loss at the Reaction Point, and target a 1:1 Risk-Reward Ratio (Ch16).

**Strategy #1 — Reactions to VWAP** — trading pullbacks to the VWAP line itself, entering in the direction of whichever side (buyers/sellers) currently controls price (Ch2).

**Strategy #2 — Reactions to 1st VWAP deviations** — trading pullbacks to the 1st deviation bands rather than the VWAP line, split into the Rotation strategy (sideways markets) and Trend strategy (trending markets) (Ch10, Ch11).

**Support becoming Resistance (and vice versa)** — a Price Action setup where a strongly-defended level, once breached, flips role and is defended again from the opposite side; used both standalone and as a VWAP confluence partner (Ch12).

**Trade Manager** — the author's free software tool automating position sizing, TP/SL adjustment, Limit order placement, breakeven/reaction-point SL moves, partial closes, and pre-news Limit order protection (Ch17).

**Trailing** — continuously migrating Stop Loss forward through a chain of valid barriers as a trend develops, to protect growing profit rather than holding a fixed SL/TP (Ch16).

**Trend-start-anchored VWAP** — VWAP anchored at the large, decisive candle marking the beginning of a new trend; benefits from combined "new entrant + trapped-trader exit" order flow pressure at the touch (Ch5).

**Volume Profile** — a histogram-style indicator showing traded volume at each price level, used here to identify heavy volume zones for VWAP anchoring and TP/SL barriers; full framework covered in the author's separate Volume Profile book/skill (Ch7, Ch12, Ch14, Ch15).

**Volume Profile Trend setup** — a Volume-Profile-based setup that trades pullbacks to a volume cluster formed within an established trend; the author's preferred confluence partner for VWAP since both favor trending markets (Ch12).

**VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price)** — the average price of an instrument weighted by traded volume at each price over a defined period; the core indicator underlying every strategy in this book (Ch1).

**VWAP based Stop Loss placement** — placing SL behind the VWAP line, a deviation, or another anchored VWAP, migrating forward through the VWAP-family chain as price advances (Ch15).

**VWAP based Take Profit** — exiting a trade a few pips before price reaches an opposing VWAP-based Support/Resistance level, from any anchor type (Ch14).

**VWAP or better** — the institutional execution instruction ("buy at VWAP or better," "sell at VWAP or better") that creates real resting order flow at the VWAP line, explaining why it functions as Support/Resistance (Ch1, Ch2).

**VWAP Rotation strategy** — Strategy #2 variant for sideways markets: enter Long off the lower 1st deviation, Short off the upper 1st deviation, targeting the VWAP line for Take Profit (Ch10).

**VWAP Trend strategy** — Strategy #2 variant for trending markets: enter pullbacks to whichever 1st deviation band is trending (upper in an uptrend, lower in a downtrend), with room to trail (Ch11).

**Weekly VWAP** — VWAP anchored at the start of the trading week (Asian session open on Monday); the author's favorite intraday reference, mainly on the 30-minute timeframe (Ch3).

**Yearly VWAP** — VWAP anchored at the first trading day of the calendar year; used for swing/long-term trading, typically on the Daily timeframe (Ch3).
